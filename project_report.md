# Task 4: Sales Data Analysis

## Objective
Analyze sales data to identify the highest-selling product and month.

## Method
Loaded the CSV into Python with pandas, removed duplicate rows, converted dates and numeric values, and removed rows with missing required fields. Summarized sales by product and month and created charts with matplotlib and seaborn.

## Findings
- Records analyzed: 100
- Total sales: 264,789.87
- Highest-sales product: Electronics (77,472.23)
- Highest-sales month: June 2024 (32,316.56)

## Conclusion
Electronics generated the most sales in this dataset. June 2024 had the highest monthly sales. The business could review which products and promotions contributed to June's result and compare future months against it.
